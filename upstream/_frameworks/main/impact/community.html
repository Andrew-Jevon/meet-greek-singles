Thank you for your interest in becoming a Meet Greek Singles Community Ambassador.

Our Community Ambassadors are the heart of our platform. They help bring people together beyond profiles — through local gatherings, shared experiences, and a warm, welcoming, and safe environment rooted in Greek culture and values.

This is a voluntary, non-paid role, built on trust, shared values, and genuine engagement with the local community. Ambassadors play an important part in nurturing meaningful human connections and helping the community grow naturally and respectfully.

For certain approved events, Ambassadors may receive event-related benefits, such as complimentary or preferential participation, or other forms of recognition, depending on the nature of the event. All details are discussed and agreed in advance, on a case-by-case basis.

If you enjoy bringing people together and celebrating Greek culture and connection, we’d love to hear from you.

What Does a Community Ambassador Do?

Community Ambassadors may:

  • Organize or co-host local meetups and small events
  • Welcome new members and encourage engagement
  • Support a positive, respectful, and safe community environment
  • Represent Meet Greek Singles in their city or region

This role does not constitute employment and does not include a salary.

Who We’re Looking For

We’re looking for people who:

  • Are reliable, kind, and community-minded
  • Enjoy bringing people together naturally
  • Share the values of authenticity and meaningful connection
  • Are active members of Meet Greek Singles

Previous event-hosting experience is helpful but not required.
